# Shots and prompts

## Plan format (show this, then wait for OK)
| Shot | Seconds | Start frame | Motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | where, who, framing, light | the one action, and how it ends | 1–2 effects |

- **One action per shot.** Three actions in one shot come out as mush.
- **Exact counts:** "exactly one robot", "one wooden plank".
- **Hidden causes stay hidden:** if the payoff is a reveal, keep that object out of frame until the reveal shot and say so in the prompt.
- **Length:** a reveal with several steps (seed drops, tree grows, windows light up) needs 8–10 s, not 6.
- **Silent shot:** "No narration, no dialogue. Sound: rain, motor whirr."

## Start-frame image prompt
`{camera and framing}. {character with its exact look, from the attached reference}. {place, from the attached reference}. {pose at the start of the action}. {light and weather}. Keep the bottom-right corner free of important detail. {style sentence}. No text, no logos.`

## Motion prompt (Frames mode)
`{camera move}. {the one action}. Ends with {exact end state}. Exactly {count} characters. {sound}. No cuts.`

## Reviewing a frame or clip
Say plainly what matches and what doesn't, name the weakest one, and propose one specific fix. Examples: "Frame 3 isn't the low angle planned; Rex is at the start of the plank, not halfway. Redo for 0 credits?" / "Shot 2 ends where shot 3 starts again; redo shot 2 so Rex stops halfway."
