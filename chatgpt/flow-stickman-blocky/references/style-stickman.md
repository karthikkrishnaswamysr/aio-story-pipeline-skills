# Style: stickman toon

## Style sentence (include once per outgoing prompt)
2.5D cartoon stick-figure animation: characters have large round white heads with simple expressive faces (dot eyes, curved eyebrows, small mouth), thin black stick limbs and a small bean-shaped body, bold black ink outlines about 3 px, flat cel shading with one shadow step, set in simple 3D-looking cartoon environments with big readable props, clean bright colours, soft daylight, no text, no logos, no realistic humans.

For an approved dusk or night scene, replace “soft daylight” with the agreed lighting. Keep one consistent time of day and lighting description across the sequence.

## Characters
- The head is the identity. Tell characters apart with **one or two simple items**: a hat, a scarf, a backpack, glasses, a coloured shirt patch or a tool they always carry. Put these in the character sheet line and repeat it in every shot the character appears in.
- Give each character a height relation ("about a head taller than Ravi") so scale holds across shots.
- Faces: say the emotion in the prompt ("wide surprised eyes, open mouth").

## Sets
- Simple shapes: block houses, round trees, flat roads, big doors, stairs, a hill, a rooftop. Clear foreground, middle and background.
- Colour sets by mood: day = blue sky, green, warm tan; night = navy and indigo with one warm light; danger = orange and red accents.

## Avoid
Realistic skin, fingers, detailed clothes, crowds of more than 4 figures, small text, signs with words, logos.

## Narrated films (voiceover over silent characters)
Use this style sentence instead, without "small mouth" and without "expressive", and show reactions with the body and head, not the face:
2.5D cartoon stick-figure animation: characters have large round white heads with simple faces (dot eyes, curved eyebrows), thin black stick limbs and a small bean-shaped body, bold black ink outlines about 3 px, flat cel shading with one shadow step, set in simple 3D-looking cartoon environments with big readable props, clean bright colours, no text, no logos, no realistic humans.
