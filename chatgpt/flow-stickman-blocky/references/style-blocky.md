# Style: blocky game-avatar world

Describe the look only in plain words. Never name a game, platform or toy brand in anything sent to Flow.

## Style sentence (include verbatim once per outgoing prompt)
3D blocky toy-avatar animation: characters have cube-shaped heads with simple printed faces (dot eyes, short line mouth), rectangular bodies and square arms and legs that swing stiffly, smooth matte plastic surfaces with no studs, bright primary colours, set in a low-poly game-world made of chunky blocks and simple shapes, soft even lighting, crisp shadows, playful toy-like motion, no text, no logos, no brand names, no user-interface overlays.

## Characters
- Identity comes from **colour blocks and one accessory**: shirt colour, trouser colour, hair block colour and one item (cap, headphones, backpack, cape, sword-free tool like a hammer or wrench).
- Write the outfit as a colour list ("yellow cube head, red shirt, blue trousers, black spiky hair block, green backpack") and repeat it in every prompt.
- Movement is stiff and bouncy: arms swing from the shoulder, jumps are springy, falls are floppy and harmless.

## Sets
- Obstacle courses, floating platforms, block towns, lava-free danger (use bouncy pads, spinning bars, slides, water pools), towers, rocket pads, block forests.
- Bright saturated palette; one sky colour per scene.

## Avoid
Game names, logos, coins with symbols, health bars, chat bubbles, usernames, on-screen text, realistic humans, studs on bricks.
